[h1]Hardcore Zombies[/h1]

Zombies can smell corpses. They follow the scent, queue up, and feed. A well-fed zombie is stronger: more health, sharper hearing and sight. When nutrition is maxed out, it turns into a sprinter.

[h2]What this mod does[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Corpse scent[/b]: a fresh corpse gives off a weak scent. As it rots the scent spreads furthest, then fades. Scent drifts with the wind, so zombies upwind smell it first.
[*][b]Queuing to feed[/b]: every corpse pile has a fixed number of seats. Extra zombies wait beside the pile instead of piling on and getting stuck in each other.
[*][b]Nutrition growth[/b]: feeding builds nutrition, which is digested over time. A zombie with full nutrition becomes a super sprinter. A zombie starving for a long time loses stats.
[*][b]Skeletonization[/b]: a corpse that has been eaten clean turns into a skeleton, and all of its equipment drops to the ground broken.
[*][b]Indoor piles[/b]: a pile inside a room only recruits from squares in that room, so zombies outside the walls are never called in.
[/list]

[h2]Background[/h2]

Zombies are cannibalistic and only eat dead zombies. So if you see a corpse nobody is eating? Be careful, it may get back up! As they feed, the nutrition from rotten flesh and the buildup of the virus make zombies stronger: tougher, faster, sharper senses, greater strength. Future updates will add non-vanilla zombie types as evolution paths.

[h2]Nutrition system[/h2]

Every zombie has two meters: satiety (0-5) and nutrition (0-5).

[list]
[*][b]Eating[/b]: eating a corpse empty refills satiety. While satiety is full the zombie cannot smell corpses and stops taking orders; only after digesting some does it look for food again.
[*][b]Digesting[/b]: every configured digestion hour, satiety -1 and converts proportionally into nutrition.
[*][b]Hunger[/b]: nutrition decays naturally each day, twice as fast while satiety is 0.
[/list]

Nutrition drives the five stats. Each time it crosses a stage (17 levels total), one stat that is not yet capped gains +1 or -1 at random:
[list]
[*]Health 0.5 → 4.0 (vanilla max 3.8, min 0.5, default 1.0)
[*]Speed 3 → 1 (1 = sprinter)
[*]Strength 1 → 5 (affects how fast doors and windows break, and raises the chance of feeding when surrounded and of taking damage when attacked)
[*]Sight 3 → 1
[*]Hearing 3 → 1
[/list]

So a zombie goes through: just fed → steadily stronger → full nutrition turns sprinter → long time without food → stats drop back stage by stage, slower and slower. Below 0.5 nutrition the stats are locked at minimum, above 4.75 they are locked at maximum.

[h2]World curves[/h2]

The sandbox "Hardcore Zombies" page controls the timescale of the whole run, not individual zombies:
[list]
[*][b]Satiety curve[/b]: start → peak → minimum, together with peak day and zero day. A zombie's starting satiety rises then falls as the game progresses.
[*][b]Nutrition curve[/b]: the same three segments (percentages) control the starting point of the five stats. Zombies are weak early, strong mid-game, weak late.
[*][b]Curve exponent[/b]: 1 = linear, 2 = quadratic. Higher values make both ends steeper.
[/list]

With default values, zombies grow stronger for the first 90 days (200% nutrition on day 90), then decline, dropping to 10% by day 360. Lower the peak day to make them weaker over time; raise the minimum to keep them dangerous late.

[h2]Performance[/h2]

In extreme cases (500+ corpses, 200+ zombies) this mod averages under 20 ms of Lua time per second, with peaks under 60 ms per second. In normal play it stays under 10 ms per second, less overhead than most HUD mods.
If you run into performance problems, make sure the mod's debug mode is off, as it causes stutter.

[h2]Known issues[/h2]

[list]
[*]1. Multiplayer is not supported yet; single player only.
[*]2. A sprinter whose nutrition drops does not revert to a normal zombie.
[*]3. Crawlers cannot feed.
[*]4. State is not persisted yet: loading a save does not restore zombie stats, they start building nutrition again.
[*]5. Debug mode (DebugMode) keeps writing logs and snapshots. Turn it off for normal play.
[*]6. Zombies in old saves may carry a corpse-pile claim from the previous session; it is invalidated automatically when you load in.
[/list]

2, 3 and 4 will be fixed in the next version. 1 is not planned for now since there is no way to test multiplayer. If you can, grab the source from [url=https://github.com/BlueGary233/HardcoreZombies]GitHub[/url] and modify it yourself.

[h2]Dependency[/h2]

[b]ZombieBuddy is required[/b]: this mod's Java bridge depends on it.

[h2]Scent parameters[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Rot delay[/b]: how many in-game hours before the smell starts, with a configurable random range.
[*][b]Full rot age[/b]: how long it takes for the scent to reach maximum strength.
[*][b]Count bonus[/b]: how many corpses stacked together maxes out the count factor.
[*][b]Radius[/b]: scent range in tiles, interpolated between min and max by rot age and count.
[*][b]Intensity[/b]: higher intensity means more focused zombies, more likely to ignore your footsteps, body scent, even gunshots.
[*][b]Weights[/b]: how much rot age and count each affect radius and intensity. The two weights are complementary.
[/list]

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hardcore Zombies[/b] page: satiety and nutrition curves (start, peak, zero day, curve exponent)
[*][b]Mod options[/b]: scent radius and volume, wind drift weight, seats per pile, feeding duration, skeletonization time, debug mode
[/list]
